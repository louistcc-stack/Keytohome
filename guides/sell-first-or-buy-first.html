Sell first or buy first when upgrading from HDB?

Both work. They fail in different ways. Here is how each one plays out, and the questions I ask before I recommend one.

By Louis Tay, Huttons Asia, 16 years in property. Updated 21 November 2026. 7 min read.

The short answer

Sell first if your cash is tight or you're buying as a single. Buy first if you're a married couple, you have the cash for 20% ABSD upfront, and you're confident your flat will sell inside the deadline.

Sell first, then buy

You sell the flat, then buy the condo while you own nothing else. No ABSD applies to Singapore citizens buying their only home.

What goes well

  • You know your exact cash and CPF before you choose a unit.
  • No ABSD, so no large sum tied up with IRAS.
  • One home loan at a time.

What can go wrong

  • The gap. HDB lets a seller who has bought a completed home stay up to 3 months after resale completion, if the buyer agrees. Beyond that you rent or stay with family.
  • Price moves. If condo prices rise while you sell, your budget buys less.

Buy first, then sell

You buy the condo while you still own the flat. A Singapore citizen buying a second home pays 20% ABSD on top of buyer's stamp duty, within 14 days.

A married couple with at least one Singapore citizen can claim that ABSD back if they sell the flat in time. IRAS counts 6 months from the purchase date for a completed condo. For a new launch, it counts 6 months from TOP or CSC, whichever comes first. IRAS says it will not extend this timeline.

What goes well

  • You move once, straight into the new home.
  • With a new launch, you can stay in the flat during construction.

What can go wrong

  • Cash flow. On a S$1.6 million condo, 20% ABSD is S$320,000, payable before your flat sale brings in a cent.
  • The deadline. Miss it and the ABSD is not refunded.
  • Two loans. The flat's loan counts in your TDSR, which shrinks the condo loan.
  • Singles can't claim the refund at all.

How I decide with clients

I ask four questions:

  1. Can you pay 20% ABSD in cash or CPF today without touching your emergency fund?
  2. How fast are flats like yours selling in your block right now?
  3. Is the condo completed or still under construction?
  4. If the flat took 6 months longer to sell, what would you do?

If the answer to the last one is "I don't know", we sell first.

Questions people ask me

Do I pay ABSD if I buy a condo before selling my HDB?

Yes, if you still own the flat when you buy. A Singapore citizen pays 20% ABSD on a second residential property. Married couples with at least one Singapore citizen can apply for a refund if they sell the flat within the IRAS deadline.

How long can I stay in my HDB flat after selling it?

If you have bought a completed home, HDB allows a temporary extension of stay of up to 3 months after resale completion, with the buyer's agreement. It ends on its own after 3 months. Any payment for the stay is agreed between you and the buyer.

What if I can't sell my HDB within 6 months?

The ABSD you paid will not be refunded. IRAS states there is no extension of the 6-month timeline. That's why I price the flat to sell and line up buyers before the deadline gets close.

Can singles buy first and claim back ABSD?

No. The ABSD refund for selling your first home applies to married couples with at least one Singapore citizen. Singles who buy a second home pay ABSD with no refund, so selling first is usually the safer route.

Is buy first better for new launches?

Often, for couples. The refund clock for an uncompleted project starts at TOP or CSC, so you can stay in the flat during construction. You still need the 20% ABSD in cash or CPF at the start.
